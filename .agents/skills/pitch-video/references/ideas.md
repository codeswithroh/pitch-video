# Stage A: video ideas from who the person is

Use this when the user asks "what video should I make?", "give me ideas", or has no script yet.
Goal: 5 ideas they could record today, each one obviously *theirs*, not generic creator advice.

## 1. Read the person first (2 minutes, no interrogation)

Look before you ask. In order:
1. What's already in the conversation and the repo they're in (what they build, what they shipped, their README, their commit history).
2. Links they give you: X / LinkedIn / GitHub / YouTube / personal site. Read the bio, the last ~20 posts, pinned repos, and which posts got the most replies.
3. Only then ask, in one message, for whatever is still missing:
   - What do you want people to do after watching? (follow, try a product, hire you, book a call, buy)
   - Where will it go? (X, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok, a submission form)
   - How comfortable are you on camera? (fine / prefer my face small / voice only)

Write down a 4-line profile and show it to them before the ideas:
```
You: <role + the specific thing you're known for, in their words>
Audience: <who already follows you and what they reply to>
Edge: <what you know or have access to that most people don't>
Goal: <the one action after watching>   Platform: <where>   Camera: <comfort>
```

## 2. Pick from formats that fit

| Format | Best for | Length | Layout | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **New tool walkthrough** ("X just launched, here's what it does") | anyone who tries new AI/dev tools early | 45–75 s | split | hook claim → 3 things it does, each shown → one catch → verdict |
| **I tried X so you don't have to** | reviewers, opinionated builders | 60–90 s | split | the promise → what happened (footage) → the surprise → who it's for |
| **Product demo / pitch** | founders, hackathon builders | 90–150 s | pitch | hook → problem → "until now" → product → proof → how it works → CTA |
| **Build-in-public update** | indie hackers | 30–60 s | split | the number → what I shipped → what broke → what's next |
| **How I built X** | engineers | 60–120 s | split or pitch | the result first → the one hard part → the trick (diagram) → try it |
| **Tutorial: do X in 60 seconds** | educators, devrel | 45–60 s | split | the end result → 3 steps, each on screen → the gotcha |
| **Hot take** | people with a strong audience reaction | 30–45 s | split | the take → the evidence (screens, numbers) → what to do instead |
| **Comparison: X vs Y** | reviewers | 60–90 s | split | the question → same task in both → the winner and why |
| **Launch announcement** | product teams | 30–60 s | split | what's new in one line → show it → who it's for → where to get it |
| **Explain a concept** | experts | 60–90 s | split, anim-heavy | the misconception → the real model (diagram) → one example |

**Split** = 9:16, footage and motion graphics on top, their face on the bottom (Reels/Shorts/TikTok/X). **Pitch** = 16:9 with a face bubble (submissions, YouTube, landing pages).

Rules for choosing:
- Ride what's happening *this week*: a launch in their niche, a trending repo, a hackathon deadline. Search for it if you can.
- Every idea needs something you can actually put on the top half: a site to film, a repo, numbers, a diagram. No visuals → drop the idea.
- Match their edge. A Solana dev reviewing a new Solana tool beats a Solana dev reviewing a photo app.
- At least one idea must be low-effort (under 60 s, no setup) so they can start today.

## 3. Present 5 ideas like this

```
1. "<working title>"   · <format> · <length> · <layout>
   Hook (first line they'd say): "<exact sentence>"
   Why you: <one line tying it to their profile/edge>
   On screen: <what fills the top half: site/repo/anim>
   Gets them: <the action from their goal>
```
Rank by (fits them) × (timely) × (easy to film). Recommend one. Then: "Want the script for #N?"
