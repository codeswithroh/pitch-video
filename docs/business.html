Questions

Good to know

I'm not good on camera. Will it look bad?

No. Talk at your normal pace. We remove the pauses and "umms" and tighten it, so you come across confident.

What phone do I need?

Any phone made in the last few years. Hold it at eye level, face a window for light, and record somewhere quiet.

How do I send the video?

Upload it to Google Drive, Dropbox or WhatsApp-to-yourself and paste the link. For big files, Google Drive works best.

How do I pay?

Your first video: after you've seen it, through secure checkout (cards, UPI and local methods, in your currency). Rush and Monthly are paid upfront.
