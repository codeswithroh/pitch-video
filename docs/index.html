Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be good on camera?

No. Read your script once, at your normal pace. We cut the pauses and tighten the pacing, so it comes out sounding confident.

Is it allowed in hackathons?

Yes. Hackathons judge your project, and the video is how you present it: your voice, your product, your words. Check your event's rules for specifics like length limits.

How do I pay?

For the launch offer, you pay after you've seen the finished video, through secure checkout (cards, UPI and local payment methods, in your currency). Rush and Founder are paid upfront.

Can I do it myself for free?

Yes. The whole pipeline is open source as an agent skill: github.com/codeswithroh/pitch-video. This service is for when you'd rather spend the evening building.
